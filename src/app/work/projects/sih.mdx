## Overview

As part of the Smart India Hackathon (Software Edition), our team developed a comprehensive platform aimed at empowering students to enhance their entrepreneurship skills and track their progress through various programs.

## Key Features

- **Robust Backend**: Featuring Google Sign-In for seamless authentication and secure data access.
- **Tracking and Analytics**: Empowering students and administrators with insightful analytics to track progress.
- **Media Upload**: Integrated capabilities for users to upload structured media.

## Technologies Used

- **Framework**: Next.js
- **Capabilities**: Google OAuth, RESTful APIs, Media Handling
